Note: Addresses are given as commonly listed. Please check them on each
college's official website before using them for anything official.

Part A (1-13): technical colleges. 1-5 come from the original list;
6-13 were added from web directories (Careers360, CollegeBatch,
Wikipedia, official brochures) in Oct 2026. Flagged PINs are where
sources disagreed.
Part B (14-41): all other colleges from the original list, women's
colleges first. Original list kept in college_list_full_backup.txt.
